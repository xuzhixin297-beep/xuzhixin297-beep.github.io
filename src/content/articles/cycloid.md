# The Cycloid

A cycloid is the curve traced by a fixed point on the circumference of a circle as the circle rolls along a straight line without slipping.

It is a fascinating curve with connections to geometry, calculus, mechanics, and the brachistochrone problem.

## Parametric equations

For a circle of radius \(r\), the cycloid can be described parametrically by:

$$
x = r(t-\sin t)
$$

$$
y = r(1-\cos t)
$$

Here, \(t\) is the angle through which the circle has rotated.

## Why is it interesting?

The cycloid appears in several famous mathematical problems, including the brachistochrone problem: finding the curve along which a particle slides under gravity in the shortest possible time.
